Partners don't just check your answer. They watch how you think, how you handle pressure, and whether you can adapt when the question changes.

How it works

Four steps, about 30 minutes.

  1. 01

    Choose a case

    Pick a category, then a case from foundation to advanced. Or practise your fit stories.

  2. 02

    Set it up

    Choose a real interview or coaching as you go, a full case or just one part, and how tough your interviewer is.

  3. 03

    Talk it through

    Answer out loud. Exhibits appear when they're shared, and the transcript follows along. Take all the time you need to think.

  4. 04

    See your results

    Get a decision, a rating on each criterion, and exactly what the next level required.

Two ways to practise

Train with a coach. Then go without one.

Switch between them at any point, even halfway through a case.

Real interview

No hints and no help, just like the day itself. Your assessment waits until the end.

Coach me as I go

After each answer, your coach offers a short tip on your thinking. Hear it, or skip it and carry on.

Your results

Assessed on your thinking, not your vocabulary.

No marks for polished phrasing, and none lost for your accent or for pausing to think. The assessment covers what interviewers care about: how you structured the problem, what you did with the data, and where you landed.

  • Rated on five problem-solving criteria
  • A level for each question, and what the next one required
  • A model answer and worked numbers once you finish

Questions

Good to know.

What do I need?

A computer with a microphone, and Chrome, Edge or Safari. There's no camera and no avatar. If you'd rather not speak, you can type your answers.

What happens if I pause to think?

Nothing at all. Nothing is timing you, and the interviewer waits until you submit your answer. Pauses never count against you.

Is this how McKinsey scores candidates?

McKinsey doesn't publish its scoring rubric, and Case Casa isn't affiliated with McKinsey. Our criteria draw on what McKinsey publicly says its problem-solving interview assesses, such as how you structure ambiguous challenges and deal with facts and data. The scale and weightings are our own.

Can I trust the score?

Treat it as a practice signal, not a prediction. It's written by AI and can be wrong, and speech recognition sometimes mishears words. It can't tell you whether you'll get an offer.

Does it replace a human coach?

No. It gives you unlimited repetitions at any hour. A coach who has made these hiring decisions brings judgement that practice alone can't.

Is my session recorded?

Your audio isn't stored by the Practice Room, but a transcript of your answers and your results are saved. In Chrome, your browser's speech recognition sends audio to Google to turn it into text.
